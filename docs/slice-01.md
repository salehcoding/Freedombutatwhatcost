## Rules

- Timeline: 2012 Damascus countryside (bombing), Ghouta chemical background 2013 kept off-screen/for later chapter. No anachronisms: no Idlib-held scenes, no Russian planes, no organized evacuations in slice 1.
- Respect guardrails: civilian dignity, no gore close-ups, no real victim names, tolls only as ranges if ever stated, soldiers as combatant threat only.

## Beats (10-minute experience)
1. Morning at home — sun, breakfast, quiet brother beside you. Carry small things for mother. Emotion: warmth, safety.
2. Sky changes — distant roar, birds scatter, father freezes at window. Follow, stay close. Emotion: unease.
3. First bombs — flashes, dust, windows shatter from pressure. Crouch under table, cover ears, hold brother. Emotion: terror.
4. Dust settles — cracked room, coughing, names called softly. Crawl, check family, hold hands. Emotion: shock, fragile relief.
5. The street — neighbors hurrying, smoke over rooftops, soldiers distant threat only. Stay low, follow adults, carry. Emotion: fear + responsibility. No fighting.
6. The decision — doorway, one small bag, keepsake choice, brother's hand. Emotion: grief + will to live.
